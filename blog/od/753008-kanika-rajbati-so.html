Cuttack Chandi Temple

କଟକ ଚଣ୍ଡୀ ମନ୍ଦିର

The Cuttack Chandi Temple is dedicated to the goddess Chandi, who is regarded as the presiding deity of the city of Cuttack, and the people of Cuttack have long worshipped her as the guardian of the city. The temple stands near the bank of the Mahanadi river. The goddess is also known as Katak Chandi, and many people of the city turn to her as their protector.

There is no written history of the temple's founding. The Odisha endowments department records a local tradition that tells of Hansa Panda, a priest of the Kanika Raja, who grazed his cattle on the land and rested there. Another account holds that the image is older than the temple that now shelters it. These stories belong to the oral tradition of the city rather than to the historical record.

The temple is best known for its festivals. Durga Puja, held in the autumn, is the main celebration, and the Chandi Temple is at the centre of the festival season in Cuttack, when processions and decorated pandals fill the old part of the city. Kali Puja, which follows soon after, is also celebrated with great fervour, and large crowds gather in the temple's lanes on these days.

The post office that holds the cancellation, Kanika Rajbati, takes its name from the palace of the Kanika royal family, the former zamindars of the Kanika estate, and the same family appears in the legend of the temple. Rajbati means the royal residence, and the name of the post office recalls a part of the city's history that is closely tied to the story of the goddess.

The pictorial cancellation at Kanika Rajbati Sub Post Office carries the temple of the city's guardian goddess to collectors across the country.
