Netaji Birth Place

ନେତାଜୀ ଜନ୍ମସ୍ଥାନ

Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose was born in Cuttack on 23 January 1897, in the family house known as Janakinath Bhawan, in the Oriya Bazar area of the old city. The house takes its name from his father, Janakinath Bose, a well known lawyer and public figure of Cuttack, and it was the family home in which Subhas spent his childhood years, one of fourteen children of the household.

As a boy Subhas studied at the Protestant European School and then at the Ravenshaw Collegiate School in Cuttack, and the city shaped much of his early outlook. Before he left for Calcutta in 1913 to study at the Presidency College, he had developed a strong interest in service, in spiritual reading and in the life of the nation. His later life took him from the Indian Civil Service examination to the leadership of the Indian National Congress, and then to the Indian National Army and the Azad Hind movement.

The old family house was restored with the help of the Indian National Trust for Art and Cultural Heritage (INTACH) and the Odisha government, and it was turned into the Netaji Birthplace Museum in the early 2000s. The museum is arranged in the rooms of the old house. Its galleries show his early life, his years in prison and the Azad Hind radio, and it displays letters, photographs and personal belongings. Visitors can walk through the rooms in which he grew up.

The museum is a place of pilgrimage for many of those who admire Netaji, and a place where school groups often come to learn about the freedom movement. Visitors should check the timings before they go, since the opening days differ from those of other museums.

The pictorial cancellation at Kazibazar Sub Post Office, in the old city of Cuttack, marks the birthplace of one of the best known leaders of the freedom movement.
