Mukteswar Temple

ମୁକ୍ତେଶ୍ୱର ମନ୍ଦିର

The Mukteswar Temple in Bhubaneswar is a small temple of Lord Shiva, but it is regarded as one of the finest works of the Kalinga style of architecture. Most scholars date it to the middle of the tenth century, in the period of the Somavamshi kings, and see it as a link between the early temples of the city, such as the Parasurameswara, and the great temples that followed, such as the Lingaraja. The name Mukteswar means the lord of liberation, and the temple is protected by the Archaeological Survey of India.

The most striking feature is the torana, an ornamental arched gateway in front of the temple, carved with figures of women, scrolls and floral designs. Archways of this kind are rare among the temples of Odisha, and the torana is often linked to earlier Buddhist traditions of stone gateways. Through it a visitor reaches the temple, which is built on a low platform, with a compact tower and a pyramid-shaped roof over the entrance hall, a form that was new at the time.

The carving on the walls is among the most delicate in the city. It includes ascetics, animals and scenes from fables, with monkeys, crocodiles and other creatures carved in a lively way, and the stone is cut with such precision that the temple is often called a gem of Odishan architecture. A smaller temple, the Siddheswara, stands beside it, and a small tank called the Marichi Kunda lies within the compound, where local belief has it that the water brings blessings to those who seek a child.

The temple is also a venue for the arts. A dance festival held here each year, usually in January, brings performers of Odissi and other classical forms to a stage in the temple's setting. The dancers and the carved figures on the walls make a fitting pair, since the sculptors of Odisha drew on the same traditions of dance and music in their work.

The pictorial cancellation is held at the Bhubaneswar Mukhya Dak Ghar, the main post office of the city, and it adds one of Bhubaneswar's early temples to the list of PPCs of the state capital.
