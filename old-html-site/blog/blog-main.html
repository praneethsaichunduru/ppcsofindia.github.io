Choose a state, narrow it by post-office type or a word, and share the link.

How to read this page: the colour shows the type of post office. ★ marks a seasonal post office, one that is open only for part of the year.

★ Seasonal post offices work only for part of the year, usually during a pilgrimage or tourist season, so a cancellation from them can be collected only then. Check that the post office is open before you plan a visit or send an article.
